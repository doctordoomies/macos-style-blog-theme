Ephemeris is a blog theme for Jekyll and GitHub Pages that turns your site into a
macOS desktop. Posts open as documents, the post list lives in Finder, and the
menu bar, Dock and Spotlight all work the way you'd expect on a Mac.

This post is a short tour. Open the others from Finder or press **⌘K** to search.

## The lock screen

Every new visit starts at the lock screen with the date and a big clock. There is
no password: click anywhere, press any key, or swipe up on a phone.

## The Dock

The Dock holds the apps. Hover it to see magnification, click an app to open it,
and minimize a window to watch it fold into the Dock with the genie effect.

| App | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Finder | Your posts as files, grouped by category tags |
| Obsidian | The same posts as notes in a vault |
| Mail | A welcome message and new-post notifications. Visitors can write to you |
| Notes | A pinned "about me" note, and notes visitors leave in their own browser |
| Terminal | Your blog as a tiny file system. Try `help`, `ls`, `cat` |
| Games | Your personal projects as app icons |
| Spotify | A music player for your favourite album |
| System Settings | Appearance, wallpaper, Dock size and more |
| Downloads | A stack of files you'd find in any developer's Downloads folder |

## Windows

Windows can be dragged, resized from any edge, snapped to the sides of the screen,
zoomed and minimized. The traffic lights go gray when a window is in the
background, just like macOS.

## Spotlight

Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux) and start typing. Spotlight
searches post titles, descriptions and categories, finds apps, and even does
quick math like `24 * 365`.

## Menu bar

The menu bar changes with the front app. On the right you'll find RunCat (it
runs faster when the page is busy), Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center, Spotlight
and the clock, which opens Notification Center.
